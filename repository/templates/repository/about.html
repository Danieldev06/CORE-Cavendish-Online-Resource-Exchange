About CORE

What is CORE?

CORE (Cavendish Online Resource Exchange) is a centralized digital repository designed to address the persistent challenges of academic resource sharing at Cavendish University Zambia.

Our Mission

To preserve institutional knowledge and make academic resources accessible to every student, breaking the "knowledge loss loop" that has historically hindered student learning and academic preparation.

Key Features
  • Resource Upload: Seniors and lecturers can upload academic resources
  • Verification Workflow: Lecturers review and approve resources before they go live
  • Search & Filter: Find resources by department, course, year, semester, and type
  • Easy Download: One-click download with download tracking
  • Role-Based Access: Different permissions for students, lecturers, and administrators
The Problem We Solve

Before CORE, students at Cavendish University relied on informal peer-to-peer networks to access academic materials. This system was inefficient, unreliable, and resulted in the permanent loss of valuable resources when students graduated.
